There are currently more than 4,700 colleges and universities that provide
online courses to their students. In 2015, more than 6 U.S. million students
were taking at least one course online; this number grew by 3.9% from the
previous year. In 2021, more than 53% of postgraduate students were taking
at least some classes online. The total number of online students in the
U.S. was 7.5 million in 2024.

Virtual education is most commonly used in high school and college. 30-year-old
students or older tend to study online programs at higher rates. This group
represents 41% of the online education population, while 35.5% of students
ages 24–29 and 24.5% of students ages 15–23 participate in virtual education.
