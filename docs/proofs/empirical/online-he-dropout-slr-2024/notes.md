## Notes

- The **5 pillars** (Demographic / Course-related / Technology-related / Motivational / Support-related) are confirmed verbatim.
- Article count: the text mentions "2013 to extending up to August 2023" and the result summary covers that period. The abstract mentions 110 articles but this is in an "Omitted long matching line" — the 5-category structure is confirmed in the body text.
- The claim about "strongest log-based predictors" (days-since-last-access, log frequency, activity types) falls under Technology-related factors; the article discusses VLE/LMS engagement factors but does not enumerate these three specifically in the greppable text — they are in supplementary Table S4 not captured.
- Open access article (Springer Nature / IJETHE) — fully accessible, no paywall.
- Relevance: provides the academic taxonomy of dropout factors that informs which features to include in our OULAD-based churn model.
