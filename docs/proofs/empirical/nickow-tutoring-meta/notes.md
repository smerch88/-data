## Notes

- The abstract of NBER Working Paper 27476 explicitly states the pooled effect size of **0.37 SD** across the meta-analysis.
- The paper covers "preK-12 tutoring experiments" — the claim of 96 RCT/quasi-experimental studies is in the full paper; the abstract confirms it is "the first systematic review and meta-analysis of these and earlier studies."
- The paper does not use the phrase "none replicate Bloom 2σ" but the 0.37 SD figure is far below the 2.0 SD Bloom benchmark, supporting that inference.
- Direct relevance to our project: positions our AI tutoring system against realistic benchmarks (~0.37 SD), not Bloom's idealized 2σ claim.
